03 — Governed, real action

It acts in the real world — on a leash you hold.

Real tools, real channels, real consequences — kept safe by your keys, two human gates, dry-run defaults, and a compliance layer that fails closed.

BYOK — your keys, your spend

Bring your own keys: your Gemini key, Claude via your Vertex AI, and your channel keys for anywhere it engages. saakshe never holds the credentials that move money or post on your behalf — you do.

Two human gates — the whole leash

Exactly two taps stand between intent and the world:

gate 1
decide
at arivu — you approve the verdict before it commits.
gate 2
publish
at kural — nothing goes live until you tap.
Dry-run by default · fail-closed

Dry-run is the default — every action is previewed before it can fire. Compliance is fail-closed: when a check is unsure, the answer is no. The witness shows you exactly what would happen.

Never writes a price column

A hard constitutional limit: saakshe never writes a price or revenue figure. It will not invent, set, or publish numbers it cannot see — that decision stays entirely yours.
