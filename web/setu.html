02 — connect

Connecting is granting, source by source.

Connecting a project never means handing over a key to everything. You grant a read scope over each source you choose — and only those. Pick what the company may look at; leave the rest dark.

The seven jacks

Each is a separate grant you can revoke at any time.

Granted, not taken

Each jack is a read scope you hand over deliberately — a URL, a repo connection, an OAuth on a social account. The company sees only what you patched in.

Revocable any time

Pull a cord and the company stops learning from that source at once. What it already learned stays cited to where it came from, so you can see exactly what to forget.

Read first, always

A patched source is read-only. Granting a source never grants an action — outward acts live on the separate channel scopes, off by default.

03 — auto-understanding

The moment a jack is patched, the company starts learning it.

As soon as the bridge opens, manas's imbibers fan out over the granted sources and build the project's first Context Pack — who you are, your voice, your visual taste, your promises, your history. This runs continuously, not once: new docs, posts and comments keep flowing in, and the Pack ticks new versions. This is what makes deep audit possible — every later act traces back to a cited source.

04 — the question queue

manas never guesses. It asks you.

When the Curator finds a contradiction or a gap, it doesn't invent an answer — it raises a question to you, as a card in saakshe right beside the gate queue. Answering teaches the company, with you as the cited source. Contradictions become onboarding, never bugs. Questions are batched and respectful of your attention — never a quiz, never blocking what can already run.

gap

No refund policy is found in any granted source. What should the company tell a customer who asks?

An answer is a teaching, not a test

You are not being quizzed. A question only appears when the company genuinely cannot tell two granted sources apart, or finds nothing where it expected something. Pick the answer, type a line, or skip it for now — whatever can already run keeps running while the question waits.

Pressed into the record

Every answer commits to memory pressed with a nameplate — SOURCE: you, founder, with the date — so the audit trail always shows the company learned it from you, on a day, and did not invent it. The next time it matters, the answer is there with your name on it.

05 — pluggable creation

Swap the hands. The taste stays.

kalai, the studio, talks to creation backends through adapters. example MCP is the first-party default — images, video, music, voice. But you can mount any backend you prefer: your own MCP server, another image API, a local pipeline.

The key architectural claim

kalai's taste layer is backend-agnostic. The Creative Director's direction, the Brand-Fidelity scoring loop, and the Compliance gate wrap around whatever backend generates the pixels or audio. Change the engine; the brand standard, the scoring, and the gate do not move.

What kalai never holds

kalai makes; it does not speak. It carries no channel keys and never publishes. When a piece is done, that is a handoff to kural, the only mouth — who only ever holds the scopes you granted on the next section.

06 — channel yoking

Each channel patched separately, with four scopes you throw.

Every channel — X · Instagram · LinkedIn, more later — connects on its own keys, and carries four independent scopes you toggle. Any combination: post-only, listen-only, everything, nothing. kural only ever holds the keys you chose to hand it.

The four scopes

  • publish  — send posts outbound.
  • reply  — answer comments outbound. Sensitive replies stay founder-gated.
  • listen  — read mentions and comments inbound, feeding manas.
  • outreach  — send DMs as you.

Throw them on the board

The live switches are in the demo above (section 03). Patch two sources to surface the question, answer it, then the channel strip appears with twelve real switches — four per channel — and a plain-words line that reacts as you flip them.

A safe default: every switch starts off. The company can learn and propose immediately, but nothing leaves a channel until you throw a switch.

Every control, in one place

Master kill switch

The bridge can always be closed — one throw greys the whole board. Nothing learns, nothing acts, until you open it again.

Per-agent enable

Switch any one of the four — manas, arivu, kalai, kural — on or off independently. Let the company learn but make nothing; let it make but say nothing.

Per-scope toggles

Twelve switches across three channels — publish, reply, listen, outreach — each thrown on its own. The board in section 03 is the real thing.

Spend caps

A daily ceiling on what the company may spend on creation and outreach. Reach it and work pauses for your tap, never overruns it.

Gate strictness

Three settings, your call: gate everything · gate only the irreversible · auto with a daily digest. Loosen as trust grows; tighten in a breath.

Data boundaries

Mark what manas may and may not read, source by source. Revoke a jack and the company stops learning from it from that moment on.

It is up to you to use these or not. setu hands you every control; the company assumes none of them on its own.

08 — always in sync · deep audit

The bridge stays open both ways. The witness records everything.

The company keeps learning, and saakshe, the witness, records which source taught it what, which permission allowed which act, and which tap approved it. The audit trail is the product's spine — not a feature. Every entry is pressed with a nameplate, so provenance is a physical artifact, not plain prose.

Inbound — the company keeps learning

New docs, fresh posts, the comments coming in through a granted listen scope — all of it flows back across the bridge into the Context Pack, which ticks new versions. The company you connected six months ago is not the company it knows today; it tracks who you have become.

Outbound — only through what you granted

Every outward act is checked against the scopes you threw and the gate strictness you set. Nothing leaves a channel it was not granted; nothing irreversible fires without a tap. The same trail that records learning records every act and the permission behind it.

09 — what's wired today

Plainly: what is wired right now.

Today, example MCP is the wired creation backend, and your own accounts are the wired channels. The self-serve adapter layer — mounting any creation backend you like — and the channel connectors as a productized, self-serve step are the work ahead. The architecture above is real and backend-agnostic by design; the productization is the next step, and we are saying so directly rather than implying it already ships.
