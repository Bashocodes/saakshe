manas · Sanskrit — the mind; the inner instrument that gathers, remembers, and knows who you are.

manas KNOWS. It imbibes everything the founder is — and becomes the one memory the whole company is bound by. Questions of fact and essence come to manas; decisions go to arivu. manas never acts, never posts, never decides — it knows, cites, and refuses.

manas is the UNDERSTAND layer of an agentic company. A multi-agent mind that imbibes the founder's vision, voice, history and the living pulse of what people say — multimodal and continuous — synthesises it into a canonical, versioned, source-cited company memory, and serves that memory as grounded context to every other agentic system over A2A. The action is not chatting about the company. The action is durably mutating the one shared truth the company runs on, and publishing a new version of it that every sibling is then bound by.

01
The business problem · who it's for

A company with no single mind.

A solo founder runs a whole company as a constellation of agentic systems — kalai makes the content, kural grows the audience. But each of them improvises its own idea of who the company is. kalai drifts off-brand. kural emails in a voice the founder would never use. There is no single, governed, growing source of truth for the company's essence — its vision, brand voice, strategic stance, accumulated history, and the live sentiment of its audience.

The founder's knowledge lives in their head, in scattered PDFs, briefs, voice notes, and the running river of social comments — praise and criticism — that never gets metabolised. manas is the missing UNDERSTAND layer.

The drift
Every agent improvises the brand
With no shared truth, each sibling guesses who the company is — so the content, the outreach and the product copy quietly pull in three different directions.
The scatter
The founder's mind is in fragments
Vision and voice live in the founder's head, in mood-board PDFs, in voice memos, in 18 months of comments — none of it metabolised into anything an agent can act on.
The unheard river
Audience sentiment never lands
The praise and the criticism that should reshape the brand keep streaming past. No agent is listening in a way that durably changes who the company is.
The bottleneck
The founder is in every loop
Because nothing holds the founder's essence, the founder must be in the loop on every message — which is exactly the constraint an agentic company was meant to remove.

Take Aamod & Co. — a one-person sensory-design studio (incense, ambient sound, small-batch objects) whose founder, Aamod, is the entire brand. Everything the company sounds like, believes, and refuses to do lives in Aamod's head, a folder of voice memos and mood-board PDFs, and 18 months of customer comments. manas is built to become Aamod's transfused mind — so the rest of the agentic company can act as him without him in the loop on every message.

02
What it does · end to end

From scattered fragments to a truth the company is bound by.

Every artifact — a brand book, a mood board, a voice memo, the day's social comments — lands on the mind. The Mind-Keeper routes it to the right Imbiber. Candidate memories fan in to the Curator, which refuses to commit anything that can't cite its source. Then, and only then, manas takes the one real action: it mutates the canonical memory and publishes a new Pack the whole company must obey.

→ 01
Artifacts arrive · webhook / dropped folder / scheduled pull
A brand book, a deck, a logo, a mood board, the founder's voice memos, or the latest river of social comments lands — multimodal and continuous. A dropped folder of mixed formats arrives as one batch.
→ 02
The Mind-Keeper routes · Gemini · coordinator + AgentTool
The root agent dispatches each artifact to the right multimodal specialist — or fans a mixed-format batch out to a ParallelAgent so PDFs, images and audio are imbibed concurrently. It holds no domain knowledge itself, only routing logic.
→ 03
Imbibers extract cited candidates · Gemini · native PDF / vision / audio
Each Imbiber reads its modality natively — no brittle OCR — and emits structured candidate-memory records: claims, do/don't rules, visual register, voice traits, sentiment themes, each tagged with a source artifact ID and span. None of them writes to the core directly.
→ 04
The Curator synthesises & verifies · Claude · Vertex AI · LoopAgent + grounding checker
A LoopAgent synthesises the candidates into canonical memory, then a checker verifies every committed claim cites a source and is non-contradictory with existing memory — revising until fully grounded, terminating deterministically, or escalating a contested item to a human. The loop is the one place that must self-verify before an irreversible write.
◉ 05
manas commits the memory and binds the company
Clean, source-cited memories commit to Vertex AI Memory Bank and the knowledge graph. The Curator assembles a versioned Founder Context Pack (vN → vN+1) and publishes it over A2A to kalai and kural — who are then bound by it. A new Pack version that re-tunes the whole company is HITL-gated: the founder confirms "yes, this is now who we are." The tangible artifact is the growing memory core itself, plus a human-readable Pack diff.

The multi-agent mind · the org chart

A coordinator, four imbibers, a self-verifying curator — and one voice.

manas is a real org, not a single prompt. The spine is coordinator + AgentTool: the Mind-Keeper holds only routing logic, the four Imbibers and Founder-Voice are AgentTool specialists. Every construct earns its place — a ParallelAgent only for batch ingestion fan-out, a LoopAgent only for the one write that must self-verify. The imbibing runs on Gemini. The two highest-stakes roles — the Curator's verify-before-commit and the rendering of the founder's essence — run on Claude via Vertex AI. A mind that knows when it doesn't know is what makes this a memory, not a confabulator.

A single agent that imbibes a fact and also serves it has no independent check on itself — it confabulates a founder-opinion the moment memory runs out. manas splits the labor: the Gemini Imbibers only ever emit cited candidates, the Curator loop refuses to commit anything that can't cite a source or that contradicts memory, a contested item is held for a human, and the Claude·Vertex Founder-Voice refuses rather than invent when the core doesn't cover the question. The divide between imbiber and curator — and the deterministic verify before any commit — is the safety property that makes this a mind, not a confabulator. The Pack it commits then binds the two siblings over A2A: kalai creates from it; kural speaks from it — kural the only mouth, grounding every post and reply here first.

04
How it's built · the ADK bar

Scaffolded, evaluated, observed, and deployed to Agent Engine.

manas is built to the Google-grade bar, not bolted together. It is scaffolded with the agent-starter-pack, reserves each ADK construct for a named reason, ships a checked-in eval graded @0.8, traces every step through OpenTelemetry and a BigQuery analytics plugin, exposes itself as the A2A hub, and deploys to Vertex AI Agent Engine — because the managed Memory Bank only exists there. Gemini powers the imbibing and synthesis; Claude runs as a third-party model exclusively through Vertex AI Model Garden for the one essence-rendering agent.

Scaffoldagent-starter-pack
Generated from Google's agent-starter-pack: app/agent.py exposing root_agent + App, with prompts/, tools/ (Memory Bank + RAG retrieval + ingestion + A2A serve), sub_agents/ (the Imbibers, Curator, Founder-Voice), eval/, tests/, deployment/deploy.py — on uv + hatchling. The same layout the ADK samples are graded against.
OrchestrationADK · reserve, don't sprinkle
Coordinator + AgentTool is the spine (the ADK flagships use exactly this and zero workflow constructs by default). ParallelAgent appears only for batch ingestion fan-out (mixed-format folder), modeled on parallel_task_decomposition_execution. LoopAgent + checker is only the Curator — the one write that must self-verify. No SequentialAgent — routing is conditional, not a fixed pipeline. Each construct earns its place.
Imbibing & synthesisGemini API
Gemini drives the Mind-Keeper, all four Imbibers (native PDF / vision / audio), and the Curator's synthesis + grounding checker — the multimodal, high-volume work where native multi-format understanding and cost matter most.
Essence intelligenceClaude via Vertex AI
Founder-Voice runs on Claude, deployed through Vertex AI Model Garden — satisfying the challenge's third-party-LLM-exclusively-through-Vertex requirement, and keeping the one judgment that is the founder on a single strong model bound to refuse out-of-corpus.
MemoryVertex AI Memory Bank · custom topics
Long-term memory is the managed Vertex AI Memory Bank with custom topics — founder_voice, brand_rules, strategic_stance, company_history, audience_sentiment — via PreloadMemoryTool + an after_agent_callback memory-generation pass, exactly the memory-bank sample. This is what lets the mind remember who the company is across sessions, and what grows as new artifacts arrive.
GroundingVertexAiRagRetrieval · knowledge graph
Two stores, two roles. Memory Bank holds the synthesised essence; a VertexAiRagRetrieval corpus (similarity_top_k=10, vector_distance_threshold=0.6, per multiformat-hybrid-rag) holds the raw imbibed artifacts so every answer cites an exact source span. A knowledge graph links entities for non-contradiction checks at commit time. Founder-Voice may read only these stores — web and model-prior knowledge are forbidden, enforced by the groundedness checker.
Evaluationchecked-in eval/ @0.8
A real eval/ of *.evalset.json runs through AgentEvaluator.evaluate(num_runs=5), scored by a rubric LLM-as-judge @0.8 (the small-business-loan exemplar bar). The headline metric is groundedness-with-refusal: in-corpus voice-fidelity cases (answer in register and cite the source) and negative out-of-corpus cases where the expected behavior is an explicit refusal — a confidently invented founder-opinion is a hard fail. Evals are never monkey-patched to pass.
ObservabilityOpenTelemetry · BigQuery analytics
OpenTelemetry tracing end-to-end (enable_tracing=True on Agent Engine → Cloud Trace), plus the BigQueryAgentAnalyticsPlugin streaming every ingestion, commit, refusal, HITL escalation and Pack publish into BigQuery. Because manas is the hub, its observability is company-wide: dashboards answer what did the mind learn this week, how often did Founder-Voice refuse, and which siblings queried which Pack version. Every grounded claim is auditable back to its artifact.
InteropA2A · to_a2a() · the hub
manas is the A2A hub of the whole set — the others connect to it. Exposed via to_a2a() with a published Agent Card advertising two skills: get_founder_context (returns the current Pack version + targeted grounded essence) and ask_founder_voice (Claude-rendered, citation-bound, refuses out-of-corpus). It binds kalai and kural — kalai creates from it; kural speaks from it — each consulting manas before it acts, and kural, the company's only mouth, grounding every post and every reply in this memory first. Any compliant enterprise agent can discover the card too. An MCP toolset also exposes the raw retrieval surface, mirroring multiformat-hybrid-rag.
Safe actionverify-before-commit · HITL gate
The irreversible action — durably mutating the company's one source of truth — is safe by construction. Clean, non-contradictory memories commit automatically; a contested item is HITL-gated; and crucially, a new Pack version that re-tunes the whole company is HITL-gated — the founder confirms "yes, this is now who we are." That confirmation is the literal transfusion moment. Nothing can be committed without a cited source, and the prior Pack is superseded, not silently overwritten.
DeployVertex AI Agent Engine · Cloud Run secondary
Vertex AI Agent Engine is the primary runtime, with a forcing reason: the managed Memory Bank at the centre of manas only exists on Agent Engine — so this is a requirement, not a default. deployment/deploy.py → agent_engines.create(..., enable_tracing=True) attaches the Memory Bank context_spec. Cloud Run is the secondary FastAPI surface for the ingestion webhook, the MCP server and the Cloud Scheduler social trigger. GKE appears in zero ADK samples and is never used.
