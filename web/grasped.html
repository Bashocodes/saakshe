01 — What it is, in one line

“You’re not a prompt engineer. You’re a creator.”

The wedge is inversion. Everyone else helps you generate; aikizi helps you understand first — it reverse-engineers media into the words and structure that made it, so the creator stays the author and the AI does the describing.

  • Decode-first — upload anything (even work that isn’t yours: a reference, a competitor, a screenshot) and get its creative DNA: style lineage, mood, composition, palette, cultural context. README.md · aikizi.com
  • The output is a prompt you can run — every decode ends in a production-ready AI prompt, so understanding converts straight into making. aikizi.com hero
  • For creators with inspiration archives — designers, photographers, artists, directors, concept artists who collect references and need to operationalize them. docs/AIKIZI-WHITEPAPER-2026.md
  • The moat is the decoder — aikizi owns the understanding layer, not just a generation wrapper. Anyone can call a model to make an image; aikizi’s asset is the taxonomy that takes one apart. brain & decode engine

04 — The brain · IZI

A company that partly runs itself.

The most unusual thing in the repo isn’t a feature — it’s an autonomous agent that operates aikizi. IZI is a Cloudflare Durable Object (~40 modules, 17,500 lines) that remembers, decides, and acts on a schedule.

  • A real memory graph — entities typed as thought / observation / perspective / connection / reference / decision / hypothesis / principle, across 14 domains, with supersedes-versioning and vector search. src/worker/brain/brain-memory-graph.ts · brain-calendar.md
  • It maintains itself — a self-monitor, a self-repair workflow, an hourly discovery loop, and scheduled jobs the agent runs on its own. brain-self-monitor · brain-self-repair-workflow · brain-discovery
  • It runs growth — an autopilot + content-calendar across 12 social accounts (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube), with a daily Telegram briefing. cron-autopilot · content-calendar-role
  • 35 cron triggers / 33 jobs keep the platform alive — decode queues, enrichment, BYOK credits, SEO, duplicate detection, memory compaction. The lights stay on without a team. wrangler.toml · src/worker/index.ts

A security scar worth noting

After a self-elevation incident, three tables (profiles · users · entitlements) got BEFORE-trigger column-lock guards — RLS is row-level, not column-level, so a row owner could otherwise flip is_admin or a billing field. A CI audit (rls_audit.py) now fails the build if a flagged write is reintroduced. The platform learned, and hardened. CLAUDE.md · scripts/security/rls_audit.py

07 — manas’s read

What the company actually is, said plainly.

  • Category: AI visual-intelligence — the understanding layer of the creative stack, where almost everyone else builds the generation layer.
  • Unfair advantage: a decoder it owns — an 8-stream, 120+-attribute taxonomy across four media types — plus an autonomous brain that runs ops. Both are hard to copy and compound with use.
  • Shape: a prosumer creative tool with an enterprise-grade backend (408 routes, 5 databases, 35 crons, native apps) carried by one founder.
  • Voice: plain, creator-first, scientific-metaphor (“Style DNA”, “Blueprints”, “Weaves”) — never hype, never “prompt engineering.”

aikizi sells understanding, not output. In a market racing to generate more, it is the rare product that pays off knowing exactly what you’re looking at — and that is the part competitors can’t cheaply clone.
